## What we do {#what}

Earthwise Analytics builds geospatial intelligence from satellite imagery, radar and machine learning.
{: .ew-intro}

We monitor land through time and extract the information that matters: where it changed, how much and when. Our first service watches mining concessions every month, through cloud and rain. Next come regional mine monitoring, water turbidity, deforestation analysis and concession due diligence.

## How we work {#how-we-work}

### Radar first

Over Indonesia, cloud hides the ground from ordinary satellite cameras for weeks at a time. Satellite radar sees through it, day and night, so our records have no seasonal gaps.

### Machine learning and new mapping models

We combine radar, optical and terrain data with machine learning and the latest geospatial models to map land cover consistently, year after year.

### Built for Indonesia

Our methods are tuned for Indonesian terrain, cloud seasons and concession records, not adapted from a generic global product.

### Honest about limits

Every result comes with what it can and cannot tell you. Where something needs checking on the ground, we say so.
